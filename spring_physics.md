# UIKitCore & QuartzCore Liquid Glass Physics & Defaults

## 1. UIKitCore Spring Physics (`_UILiquidLensViewVariantSpec`)
- **Lift / Press Spring**:
  - `dampingRatio`: `0.625`
  - `stiffness`: `540.0`

## 2. `CASDFGlassHighlightEffect` & `CASDFKeyFillHighlightEffect` Defaults (`QuartzCore`)
- `height`: `20.0`
- `curvature`: `1.0`
- `keyAngle`: `+π/2`
- `fillAngle`: `-π/2`
- `spread`: `π`
- `amount`: `0.5`
